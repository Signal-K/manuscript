[[18 November 2024]]:
New projects:
- **Muon Hunters**: Classify muon patterns in particle detector data to study cosmic rays.
- **MicroBooNE Projects**: Identify neutrino interactions in Fermilab particle detector images.
- **Gravity Spy**: Classify glitches in LIGO gravitational wave detector data to improve signal accuracy.
- **Galaxy Zoo**: Analyze galaxy shapes to understand the universe's structure and dark matter distribution.
- **Radio Galaxy Zoo**: Match radio emissions to optical galaxies, aiding high-energy physics studies.
- **Solar Stormwatch**: Track solar storms in spacecraft images to study space weather and plasma physics.
