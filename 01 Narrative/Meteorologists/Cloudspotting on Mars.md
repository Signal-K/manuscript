# Mission brief
We will transcend from Cloudspotting on Mars to CoM: Shapes once clouds have been mapped. That's a good order of ops where the main subject (cloudspotting) doesn't have any prerequisite.

In #Chapter-2 of #Cloudspotting , users will be shown a list of #PlanetHunter candidates of the #Terrestrial `planetType`, and will match them up there.
(So we can say that #Chapter-1 of a project is about the classification process, #Chapter-2 is about matching and combining, and then #Chapter-3 would maybe be dedicated to resource/item manipulation, construction or growth).

In #Chapter-2 , users also add a picture from #Planet4 to the #Cloudspotting project, creating a little terrarium. They describe the type of clouds and this then points to surface/soil types. 

In #Chapter-3 , users can then combine the planets in #PlanetHunter with the clouds, and we can then implement #Picaso or #Pandexo 

Create resource sites/landmarks (new entities/anomalies in the table) from these discoveries. Cloud sites which point to landmarks become those structure/settlement areas
