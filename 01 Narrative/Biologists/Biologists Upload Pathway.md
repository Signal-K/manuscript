# Mission list
Bottom line, we start off with a new opening that shows users different biomes, allowing them to see which biomes have been completed by them. Each biome will have a set of things to do to add to it, like finding animals, etc.
Then, maybe #Astronomers or #Meteorologists can extrapolate this/compare it to planet candidates' landmarks. This does mean, however, that either we focus much more on adding more content to the #PlanetGenerator, or we get similar projects to #Planet4 that work on a larger range of biome types.

Let's start by having a biome/ #Terrarium  completion mission, and then have the option for users to manually make classifications of owls or the other animals. We'll keep the current options, add an upload button, and add a top-level mission titled "explore".

This does provide another question, though - can we do something with the background's visual effects?

## Blockers
I think a good starting point would be users have to do #read classifications to earn #Stardust to perform uploads. For every #Stardust they have, they can upload `x` amount of times.

## Minigames
Perhaps we implement the fishing-style games here, where users go fishing, they discover something, and they have to upload a picture of some fish/water-based anomaly (this is where AI would come in).
Obviously we'd have alternate minigames for non-aqueous anomalies/fauna
