#sunspot 

Will show "sun health", each week a new series of sunspots will drop. We'll have a reward of "Stardust", which will be distributed at the end of every week according to how many users classify the sunspots.

Components that will make up the solar health #section:
1. Sun - 3js component. Sunspots would appear on it. Later, we can have solar flares (links in with future monitoring system)
2. Reward pool & number of participating users
3. Description of task

Begin button

And then, obviously, we have the classification mechanic. This will open a new link, but we should have a unique layout and ideally not introduce any loading time.

Perhaps we can do different solar systems later. Also, this could be a good model for finding TIC objects...in terms of pointing the telescope. 

We could have a section that shows lightcurves (based on period surveys) for discovered planets.

There needs to be more interactivity, though
