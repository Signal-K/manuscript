### **🌍 Biome Classification Based on AI4Mars, PlanetFour, and Cloudspotting Data**

Each biome is influenced by:

1. **AI4Mars classifications (surface terrain analysis)**
2. **PlanetFour classifications (polar/surface anomalies, dune formations, etc.)**
3. **Cloudspotting classifications (cloud type, density, color, layering effects)**
4. **Planet properties** (mass, radius, temperature)

---

## **📜 Full Biome List with AI4Mars, PlanetFour, and Cloudspotting Combinations**

Below is a list of biomes, their formation conditions, temperature ranges, and planet size restrictions.

---

### **1️⃣ Basaltic Highlands**

- **AI4Mars:** Jagged terrain, large rocks, high elevation.
- **PlanetFour:** Craters and fault lines.
- **Cloudspotting:** Sparse cirrus clouds, minimal coverage.
- **Mass/Radius:** 0.5-3.5 Earth masses, 0.8-1.5 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 200K-350K (varies with atmosphere).

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Volcanic activity solidifying basalt flows.
- Erosion-resistant highland formations.
- Thin atmosphere → minimal cloud formation.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Dark gray/black terrain.
- Cracked lava fields, sharp elevation changes.
- Crater rims exposed in dry environments.

---

### **2️⃣ Sandy Dune Fields**

- **AI4Mars:** Fine-grain sand, low rock content.
- **PlanetFour:** Linear and star dunes.
- **Cloudspotting:** Thin cirrostratus with occasional stratocumulus.
- **Mass/Radius:** 0.6-2.0 Earth masses, 0.9-1.8 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 250K-350K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Wind deposition shaping dunes over time.
- Low moisture retention → shifting terrain.
- Sparse cloud cover → high temperature variance.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Golden brown to red-orange sand.
- Longitudinal and crescent-shaped dunes.
- Minor surface cracks from seasonal changes.

---

### **3️⃣ Polar Permafrost Flats**

- **AI4Mars:** Flat terrain, high reflectivity, mixed ice deposits.
- **PlanetFour:** Seasonal CO₂ frost detection, cratered periphery.
- **Cloudspotting:** High-altitude cirrus clouds, persistent cover.
- **Mass/Radius:** 0.8-2.5 Earth masses, 1.0-1.8 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 150K-270K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Sublimation cycling → seasonal freezing/thawing.
- Ice deposition preserving ancient impact scars.
- High atmospheric pressure → dense fog layers.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Bright white/blue patches.
- Darker terrain around sublimation pits.
- CO₂ and water ice mixture forming fractal-like erosion.

---

### **4️⃣ Sulfuric Plains**

- **AI4Mars:** High rock density, fractured terrain.
- **PlanetFour:** Cratered with possible subsurface volcanic activity.
- **Cloudspotting:** Dense stratocumulus and sulfuric haze.
- **Mass/Radius:** 1.2-4.0 Earth masses, 1.2-2.0 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 400K-800K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Atmospheric sulfur precipitation.
- Volcanic venting adding thick cloud layers.
- Crustal shifts forming deep fissures.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Bright yellow and orange deposits.
- Deep cracks with molten rock visible.
- Cloud shadows affecting ground temperature distribution.

---

### **5️⃣ Subglacial Ocean World**

- **AI4Mars:** Ice-covered terrain, no significant rock exposure.
- **PlanetFour:** Sublimation pits, layered ice.
- **Cloudspotting:** Thick cirrus with occasional stratiform layers.
- **Mass/Radius:** 2.0-7.5 Earth masses, 1.2-2.0 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 130K-280K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Tidal heating melting subsurface ice.
- Thick ice sheets preventing liquid loss.
- Insulated atmosphere trapping heat in certain regions.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Bright white to blue-tinged ice.
- Deep cracks where sublimation occurs.
- Occasional dark patches from expelled minerals.

---

### **6️⃣ Acidic Rainforest**

- **AI4Mars:** High organic material detection, silty soil.
- **PlanetFour:** Low crater density, evidence of runoff channels.
- **Cloudspotting:** Thick cumulonimbus, high precipitation rate.
- **Mass/Radius:** 1.5-5.0 Earth masses, 1.2-2.0 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 250K-400K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- High atmospheric pressure allows liquid retention.
- Precipitation recycling organics in soil.
- Continuous storms modifying landscape.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Dark green to reddish hues.
- High reflectivity from moisture.
- Dense fog covering valleys.

---

### **7️⃣ High-Altitude Alpine Ridges**

- **AI4Mars:** Steep rocky terrain, minimal sediment.
- **PlanetFour:** Erosion-resistant formations, cratered.
- **Cloudspotting:** Orographic clouds forming above peaks.
- **Mass/Radius:** 1.0-3.0 Earth masses, 0.9-1.5 Earth radii.
- **Temperature:** 180K-300K.

**Formation Mechanisms:**

- Tectonic uplift shaping sharp peaks.
- Strong wind erosion smoothing lower slopes.
- Cloud-induced precipitation forming micro-glaciers.

**Texture/Colors:**

- Dark brown to light gray.
- Shadowed valleys, rugged surfaces.
- Mist forming where moisture condenses.

### **🌐 Biome Combinations & Their Effects**

Each classification affects the biome outcome as follows:

|**Classification**|**Effect on Biome**|
|---|---|
|**Cloud Classification**|Controls moisture, temperature regulation, and erosion effects.|
|**AI4Mars Surface Type**|Determines terrain structure, soil composition, and habitability.|
|**PlanetFour Features**|Adds erosion patterns, cratering levels, and seasonal changes.|

For example, if a planet has:

- **2 cloud classifications (Cumulonimbus + Cirrus)**
- **1 AI4Mars classification (Large Rocks + Soil)**
- **Peaks in cloud density + blue coloration**

It might form a **Volcanic Wetland**, where:

- **Cirrus clouds** → regulate long-term cooling.
- **Cumulonimbus clouds** → add heavy precipitation.
- **Large rock formations** → suggest recent lava flow solidification.
- **Blue coloration** → suggests mineral-rich surface chemistry.
